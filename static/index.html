Clinical Reports

Single-agent AI systems converge on the same answer and let hallucinations slip into medical reports. CONSILIUM uses a panel of role-isolated specialist agents — ophthalmologist, optometrist, pharmacist — coordinated by a director and audited by an adversarial critic. The result: safer, more diverse, verifiable screening-support reports.

Screening support only — never a diagnosis. Requires clinician review.
